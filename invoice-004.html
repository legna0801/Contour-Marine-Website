Custom Logo Design & Animated Intro Designed a custom branded logo graphic for Contour Marine. Multiple rounds of editing were performed to ensure the final sequence was seamless with no visible glitches or blending errors between the intro and the live footage. Multi-round edit $100

Precisely synchronized the music to the video timeline so the track starts, cuts, and flows naturally across the full duration. Audio repair + sync $105

Performed frame-by-frame quality review on each revision to catch issues before delivery. Final output reviewed for visual continuity, audio consistency, and overall professional finish. QA + final cut $65

Video Upscaling to 4K & Format Preparation Both finished videos were upscaled to 4K resolution to ensure the highest possible visual quality when embedded and played back on modern displays.
